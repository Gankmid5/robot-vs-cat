Privacy Policy for Robot vs Cat: Backyard Showdown

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Robot vs Cat: Backyard Showdown (the "Application"), developed as a standalone arcade game. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This policy describes how the Application handles user information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Robot vs Cat does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or device data.

  • No personal identification data (such as names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses) is collected.
  • No location data (neither precise GPS nor coarse network location) is requested or accessed.
  • No device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID, or MAC address) are collected.
  • No cookies, analytic tracking SDKs, or telemetry tools are embedded into the game.

2. Offline Gameplay & Local Storage

The Application is designed to function entirely offline. Any game state data (such as local sound volume preferences or match high scores) is stored solely in your device's browser/local sandbox storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing application storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Permissions Requested

The Application does not require or request sensitive Android permissions:

  • No Camera or Microphone Access
  • No Contacts, Storage, or SMS Access
  • No Background Location Access

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The Application does NOT contain third-party ad networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.) and does NOT utilize third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics). There is zero data sharing with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

The Application is suitable for all ages (rated for Everyone / PEGI 3) and complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

Because the Application operates client-side without collecting or transmitting user data over external networks, your data is inherently protected against external interception.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
